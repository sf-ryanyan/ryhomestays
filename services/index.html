Owner-paid expenses

What Owners Pay Separately.

Approved property-related costs are paid by the owner and are not part of our management fee.

  • Booking platform fees
  • Cleaning and laundry
  • Utilities and internet
  • Household supplies
  • Repairs and maintenance
  • Furnishings and replacements
  • Permits, taxes, and insurance
  • Other approved third-party costs

Service pricing and availability are subject to property review and a signed agreement. Setup work, professional photography, and extended on-site support may be quoted separately.
