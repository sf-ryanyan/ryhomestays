## 🏡 About the Service · 关于服务

RY Homes Management is a short-term rental co-hosting service run by Ryan Yan in the San Francisco Bay Area. It takes on the day-to-day work of hosting so property owners don't have to: listing setup, pricing, guest messages, cleaning turnovers, maintenance coordination, and clear monthly reports.

RY Homes Management 是 Ryan Yan 在旧金山湾区经营的短租联合托管服务，替屋主承担短租的日常工作：房源设置、定价、客人沟通、清洁换房、维修协调，以及清晰的月度报告。

The approach comes from real operating experience, not revenue projections. Ryan also works with long-term rentals, so the advice is honest — including when Airbnb isn't the right fit for a property.

我们的方法来自真实的运营经验，而不是收益预测。Ryan 同样有长租管理经验，所以给出的建议是客观的——包括当你的房子并不适合做 Airbnb 时，也会直说。

**Scope of work · 负责的工作**

`Setup & furnishing 布置与家具` `Guest supplies 客用品` `Listing launch 房源上线` `Pricing & reservations 定价与预订` `Guest communication 客人沟通` `Cleaning & vendors 清洁与服务商` `Maintenance 维修跟进` `Expense reporting 支出报告`

**Takeaway for owners · 给屋主的启示**

A short-term rental can earn significantly more than a long-term lease, but it also carries higher operating costs and more daily work. The right decision comes from comparing net results, not gross revenue — and that comparison is part of every property review.

短租的收入可能明显高于长租，但运营成本更高，日常工作也更多。正确的决定应该比较净收益，而不是总收入——这也是每次物业评估都会帮屋主做的对比。

<sub>The long-term figure is the previous monthly rent. The short-term figure is average monthly gross booking revenue in the first year of operation, including guest-paid cleaning fees and before operating expenses. It is not net profit. Results vary by property, and past results do not guarantee future performance.<br>长租数字为此前月租金；短租数字为运营第一年的平均每月总预订收入，包含客人支付的清洁费，未扣除运营成本，并非净利润。结果因房而异，过往业绩不代表未来收益。</sub>
