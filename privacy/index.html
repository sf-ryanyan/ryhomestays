Privacy Policy

Information we collect

When you submit a property review request, we collect the details you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number (optional), property city and ZIP code, property type, current rental status, and any message you include.

How we use it

We use this information only to review your inquiry, contact you about it, and discuss possible services. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

What we don't ask for

We do not ask for bank statements, Social Security numbers, government ID, or other sensitive documents through this website.

How it is stored

Submissions are sent over a secure HTTPS connection and stored in a private account accessible only to RY Homes Management. We keep inquiry records only as long as needed to respond and for reasonable business records.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete the information you submitted at any time by sending a request through our property review form.

Website basics

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These services may process basic technical data, such as your IP address, to deliver the page. Your language choice is saved in your browser so the site remembers it. We do not use advertising trackers.

Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows the latest version.
