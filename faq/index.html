What is Airbnb co-hosting?

Co-hosting means getting help with agreed hosting tasks — such as listing management, guest messaging, and vendor coordination — while you keep ownership of your property.

Do I keep ownership and control?

Yes. Listing permissions, decisions, and finances are defined in our written agreement.

Who receives booking payments?

Payouts follow the booking platform's payout options and our agreement. The exact arrangement is confirmed during onboarding.

Are cleaning and repairs included?

No. Third-party cleaning, repairs, supplies, and other approved property costs are paid separately by the owner.

Can you guarantee rental income?

No. Results depend on location, property condition, market demand, regulations, pricing, seasonality, and operating costs. Past results do not guarantee future income.

Can any home become an Airbnb?

Not always. Local rules, permits, HOA restrictions, and insurance need to be reviewed first.

Can you take over my existing listing?

Yes, in most cases — after a quick review of your listing and service needs.

How are your fees set?

Fees depend on your property and the services you need, and every detail is documented in the management agreement. We'll walk you through them during your property review.

Do you clean or repair in person?

We coordinate qualified local providers, who perform the physical cleaning and repair work.

What happens after I submit the form?

We review your details. If the property is within our service area and looks like a good fit, we'll contact you to discuss next steps.
