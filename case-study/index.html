The Background

Property preparation began in late 2025, and the rental launched in December 2025. The work covered furnishing and supply coordination, listing preparation, pricing, guest communication, cleaning management, and expense tracking.

The Challenge

Launching from an unfurnished home takes far more than creating a listing. It meant sourcing supplies, setting up a guest-ready space, building operating procedures, organizing vendors, and managing reservations from day one.
